# Transfer Compatibility Policy

## 1. Compatibility Evaluation
Before knowledge transfer between assets, the platform evaluates:
- Category compatibility.
- Sensor semantic and unit match.
- Operating principle and capacity similarity.
- Manufacturer/model family alignment.

## 2. Transfer Modes
Supported modes range from `NO_TRANSFER` to `INDIVIDUAL_ONLY_LEARNING` and `MODEL_FAMILY_TRANSFER`.
