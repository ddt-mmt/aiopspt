# PHASE 87.26 — EXECUTION REPORT

## Equipment Discovery & Cross-Manufacturer Intelligence

### 1. Executive Summary
Phase 87.26 builds upon the AIOps V12 foundation established in Phase 87.25 by implementing rigorous **Equipment Discovery, Equipment Identity Resolution, Manufacturer Knowledge Integration, and Hierarchical Cross-Manufacturer Adaptive Learning**.
It ensures that the platform accurately accounts for the seven monitored assets (`ME_UPS1-3` and `ME_PAC1-4`), resolves their identities with strict provenance tracking, maps them to authoritative engineering standards, and introduces a six-level hierarchical learning architecture with selective transfer learning and negative transfer prevention.

### 2. Core Modules Implemented
- **Equipment Discovery & Identity Resolution:** Asset register mapping for UPS and PAC units with explicit verification statuses.
- **Manufacturer Knowledge Integration:** Integration with EKB standards (IEEE 1188, ASHRAE) without unauthorized fabrication.
- **Hierarchical Learning Architecture:** 6-level progression from global down to individual device intelligence.
- **Transfer & Negative Transfer Policies:** Compatibility scoring and isolation mechanisms to prevent negative transfer.
- **Onboarding & Device Profiles:** Standardized workflows for new equipment registration and digital profile versioning.

### 3. Conclusion & Status
Phase 87.26 is successfully completed within development staging bounds. Production V11 remains untouched and fully operational.
