# Manufacturer Knowledge Integration

## 1. Integration Scope
Binds discovered equipment attributes to the Equipment Knowledge Base (EKB) established in Phase 87.25.

## 2. Standards & Provenance
- Incorporates IEEE 1188 for VRLA battery maintenance intervals.
- Incorporates ASHRAE thermal guidelines for PAC operating envelopes.
- Rejects unverified or unsupported manufacturer generalizations.
