# UPS Manufacturer Research Catalog

## 1. Scope & Target Inventory
Covers known UPS assets (`ME_UPS1`, `ME_UPS2`, `ME_UPS3`). Where specific manufacturer and model identifiers are pending field verification in staging, records are categorized as `MODEL_IDENTIFICATION_REQUIRED`.

## 2. UPS System Specifications
- **Topology:** Double-conversion online UPS.
- **Capacity:** Rated power factor 0.9 / 1.0, capacity tiered by staging inventory.
- **Operating Voltage & Frequency:** Nominal 220V/230V/400V AC, 50/60Hz input/output.
- **Temperature Range:** 0°C to 40°C (recommended 20°C to 25°C for battery longevity).

## 3. Battery System & Chemistry
- **Chemistry:** Valve-Regulated Lead-Acid (VRLA) / Lithium-Ion options.
- **Design Life:** 5 to 10 years (VRLA at 25°C reference temperature).
- **Standards:** IEEE 1188 (VRLA maintenance, testing, and replacement).

## 4. Operational Telemetry
- Battery temperature, voltage, current, runtime remaining, load percentage, input/output voltage and frequency, charger status, alarm history.
