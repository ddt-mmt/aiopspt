# PAC Manufacturer Research Catalog

## 1. Scope & Target Inventory
Covers known Precision Air Conditioning assets (`ME_PAC1`, `ME_PAC2`, `ME_PAC3`, `ME_PAC4`). Where exact manufacturer models are pending field verification, status is recorded as `MODEL_IDENTIFICATION_REQUIRED`.

## 2. Equipment Characteristics
- **Cooling Capacity:** Rated sensible and total cooling capacity.
- **Refrigerant:** R410A / R134a / R32 (verified per unit specification).
- **Compressor Type:** Scroll / Inverter compressor.
- **Operating Limits:** Return air temperature 18°C to 30°C, relative humidity 40% to 60%.

## 3. Compressor Lifecycle & Standards
- **Standards:** ASHRAE handbooks for data center environmental controls, ISO standards for refrigeration systems.
- **Overhaul Interval:** Typically 40,000 to 60,000 operational hours or per manufacturer maintenance bulletin.

## 4. Operational Telemetry
- Compressor status, discharge pressure, suction pressure, discharge temperature, return air temp/humidity, current, power consumption, runtime hours, start/stop cycles, alarm history.
